# What to breed next

At some point the game stops telling you what to breed. Sighted players are expected to look at each monster's element symbols (the game calls them sigils) and work out the combinations themselves, and we can't see those symbols. So this guide holds your hand a bit: how breeding decides what you get, what to breed next on each of the first islands, and how long each kind of egg takes.

The combinations come from fan-made breeding charts, checked against each other, and from the game's own tips. Big Blue Bubble doesn't publish exact odds, so nobody outside the company knows the real percentages. Where I say something is likely or unlikely, that's from players' experience, not official numbers. The game can also change things in an update, so if something here doesn't work, let me know.

## How breeding works

- Every monster is made of elements. The five Natural elements are Plant, Cold, Air, Water and Earth.
- A baby gets its elements from its parents. Breed a one-element monster with a different one-element monster and you can get the two-element monster that has both. For example, Noggin (Earth) and Toe Jammer (Water) can give you Fwog (Earth and Water).
- It isn't guaranteed. Often you get a copy of one of the parents, or a monster with only some of the parents' elements. That's normal. Breed the same pair again.
- Your parents aren't used up. Both stay on the island, so you can breed the same pair as often as you like.
- The order doesn't matter. Noggin with Toe Jammer is the same as Toe Jammer with Noggin.
- Both parents must be at least level 4. Feed them food from the Bakery (select the monster, then FEED) to level them up. The breeding list tells you when a monster isn't ready, for example "Requires Level 4".
- Some monsters only unlock at a certain player level, even if you have the right parents. The Book of Monsters (M, then Book of Monsters) says "unlocks at level" for those.
- Each island is missing one Natural element, so not every monster lives on every island. Plant Island has no Air, Cold Island has no Earth, Air Island has no Plant, Water Island has no Cold, and Earth Island has no Water.

## How the mod helps

- The breeding lists say each monster's elements, for example "Fwog, Earth and Water", and the second list says which monsters will "breed with" the one you picked first. Put the elements of your two picks together and you know the best you can get: Fwog (Earth and Water) with Mammott (Cold) can give T-Rox (Earth, Water and Cold).
- I (fallback Control+Shift+I) on a selected monster says what it looks like and its elements. That's the sigil information sighted players get.
- When breeding finishes, the Breeding Structure screen says "You've bred (monster)", so you always find out what you got.
- The Book of Monsters says which monsters you've collected and which you haven't, so you can see what's left to breed.

## The breeding ladder

Every Natural island works the same way, one step at a time:

1. One-element monsters. Buy these in the Market. They are the building blocks.
2. Two-element monsters. Breed two different one-element monsters.
3. Three-element monsters. Breed a two-element monster with a one-element monster it doesn't share an element with. There are usually three ways to get each one, and any of them works.
4. The four-element monster. Breed a three-element monster with the one-element monster that adds the missing element, or two two-element monsters that together cover all four. Breeding the island's three-element monster with its matching one-element monster is a good habit, because you probably already have those.
5. The Ethereal monster. Each Natural island has one Ethereal monster you can breed there: pair the four-element monster with any three-element monster from the same island. This is a long shot. Players report it taking many tries, so don't be discouraged.

When you run out of ideas, find the next rung you haven't finished on the island you're on. The Book of Monsters can confirm what you're missing.

## Breeding times tell you what's coming

Every kind of monster takes its own time to breed, so the time on the Breeding Structure gives you a clue before the egg is revealed. These are the normal times. The Enhanced Breeding Structure, bought with Diamonds, cuts them by a quarter.

- 5 seconds: Noggin.
- 1 minute: Toe Jammer.
- 2 minutes: Mammott.
- 2 hours: Potbelly.
- 4 hours: Tweedle.
- 30 minutes: a two-element monster made only from Noggin, Toe Jammer and Mammott (Drumpler, Fwog, Maw).
- 8 hours: any other two-element monster, and T-Rox.
- 12 hours: a three-element monster.
- 24 hours: a four-element monster.
- 36 hours: an Ethereal monster. If you see this, celebrate!
- Odd times like 6 hours, 10 hours 30 minutes or 15 hours 30 minutes: probably a Rare. Rares only show up during events (see below).

So if you were hoping for a four-element monster and the timer says 12 hours, you got a three-element monster instead, and you can start planning your next try.

## Plant Island

This is where you start. Its elements are Plant, Cold, Water and Earth.

One-element (buy in the Market): Noggin (Earth), Mammott (Cold), Toe Jammer (Water), Potbelly (Plant).

Two-element:
- Drumpler (Earth, Cold): Noggin and Mammott.
- Fwog (Earth, Water): Noggin and Toe Jammer.
- Maw (Water, Cold): Toe Jammer and Mammott.
- Shrubb (Plant, Earth): Potbelly and Noggin.
- Furcorn (Plant, Cold): Potbelly and Mammott.
- Oaktopus (Plant, Water): Potbelly and Toe Jammer.

Three-element:
- T-Rox (Earth, Water, Cold): Noggin and Maw, or Toe Jammer and Drumpler, or Mammott and Fwog.
- Pummel (Plant, Earth, Water): Potbelly and Fwog, or Noggin and Oaktopus, or Toe Jammer and Shrubb.
- Clamble (Plant, Earth, Cold): Potbelly and Drumpler, or Noggin and Furcorn, or Mammott and Shrubb.
- Bowgart (Plant, Water, Cold): Potbelly and Maw, or Mammott and Oaktopus, or Toe Jammer and Furcorn.

Four-element:
- Entbrat (Plant, Earth, Water, Cold): T-Rox and Potbelly, or Clamble and Toe Jammer, or Bowgart and Noggin, or Pummel and Mammott, or Shrubb and Maw, or Oaktopus and Drumpler, or Furcorn and Fwog.

Ethereal:
- Ghazt (Plasma): Entbrat with T-Rox, Bowgart, Clamble or Pummel.

Special: Shugabush, the Plant Island legendary, comes from Bowgart and Clamble. It's rare and takes 35 hours.

## Rares, Epics and events

- Rare and Epic versions of monsters can only be bred during limited-time events, when the game announces that a Rare or Epic is available. Outside those events you can't get them, no matter what you breed.
- A Rare uses the same combination as the normal monster, so when a Rare is available, just breed the normal combination. Its breeding time is longer than usual, which is how you know you got lucky.
- An Epic uses its own special combination, which changes from monster to monster. Events usually say what to breed, and fan sites list the pairs if you want to look one up.
- Seasonal monsters (Punkleton, Yool, Schmoochle and others) are bred on the Seasonal Shanty during their holidays.
- Mythical monsters like G'joob (Plant Island: Pummel and T-Rox) only show up during their own events.

## Improving your chances

- Wishing Torches unlock at level 12. A lit torch improves your chances of breeding special monsters, and the more lit torches an island has, the better. You light them with Diamonds, and your friends can light them for you when they visit.
- The game's tips say that higher-level monsters improve breeding chances, so it's worth feeding your parents beyond level 4.
- More Breeding Structures (from the Market) let you try several pairs at once, and the Enhanced Breeding Structure makes every breed a quarter faster.
- Above all, be patient. The rarer the monster, the more tries it takes. Every failed breed still gives you a monster you can place, sell or feed.
