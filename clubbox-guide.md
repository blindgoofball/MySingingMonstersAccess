# The Clubbox

The Clubbox is a limited-time event that comes back every so often. Club manager Autotuna (voiced by T-Pain) asks you to build up Hype for a band of monsters, called an Act, by turning in Clubbox Tickets. The more Hype you build, the more rewards you unlock: new performers, costumes, stage decorations, eggs and more. Each round lasts about 16 days.

Almost everything in the Clubbox is a picture, so this guide explains what's on each screen and how the mod reads it. The numbers come from my own first round (the LVX LVMENZ act, on Plant Island). Other acts have different performers and rewards, and Big Blue Bubble can change things in an update, so if something here doesn't match what you hear, let me know.

## Getting there

- When a new round starts, an event screen pops up: "A new Clubbox Event has begun!" Press GO NOW to go straight there.
- Any other time, Tab to the Clubbox button on your island. It says how long the round has left and how many tickets you have, for example "Clubbox, 15d 23h left, 0 tickets". Press Enter on it.
- The first time, Autotuna walks you through choosing an act and the island it plays on. The mod reads his lines and says which act or island is selected as you press Left and Right.

Once you're there, A (or Control+Shift+A) gives you a summary: the act, its island, your Hype towards the next reward, the total Hype you've made, your Clubbox Tickets and the time left.

## The Clubbox island

Tab on the Clubbox island gives you:

- EXIT: back to your own island.
- HELP and INFO: the game's own explanations.
- HYPE: the Hype game, where you spend tickets.
- CUSTOMIXER: change your performers' volume and costumes and the stage decor. It only appears once you've built enough Hype to unlock it.
- Hype progress: how much Hype you have towards the next reward, for example "Hype progress, 120 of 630". Enter opens the list of rewards.
- Clubbox Tokens: your ticket count.

## The Hype game

This is where you turn tickets into Hype. There are two wheels:

- The left wheel gives you an amount of Hype.
- The right wheel multiplies it by 1, 3 or 5.

You choose how many tickets to spend with Fewer tickets and More tickets. In my round the choices were 1 or 3 tickets, and pressing More tickets at 3 went back round to 1. Each time you change it, the mod says what the wheels can give:

- 1 ticket: the left wheel gives 20, 40 or 80 Hype.
- 3 tickets: the left wheel gives 60, 120 or 240 Hype.

Spending more tickets at once doesn't change your luck, it just multiplies everything. The wheels look like they favour the small numbers: the left wheel has four 20s, three 40s and only one 80, and the right wheel has four times 1, three times 3 and one times 5. The game decides the result, though, so I can't promise any odds.

Press "Spin with 3 tickets" (or however many you chose) to spin. When the wheels stop, the mod says the result, for example "Result: 120 times 3, 360 Hype!"

The number on the dial is how many tickets you're about to spend, not how many you own. If you have 0 tickets, the dial still says 1 or 3, but you can't spin. A tells you how many you really have.

When a spin fills the Hype bar, you get that reward straight away. Some rewards bring up a screen of their own, such as "You received: 4 Clubbox Tickets", a sticker pack to open, or a new performer joining your act. These screens are tapped anywhere to continue, and the mod gives each of them a Continue button.

## Getting more tickets

From what I've seen so far:

- Some of the Hype rewards are Clubbox Tickets.
- Tapping your ticket counter offers to sell you more with real money ("Would you like to top up your Clubbox Tickets now?"). You don't have to; Close or Escape gets rid of it.

There are probably other ways too, such as daily rewards or offers in the Market. If you find one, tell me and I'll add it here.

## Hype progress

Press Enter on Hype progress to see the reward path. It opens with your total Hype, how many rewards you've earned, the time left and the act's top prizes. Then Tab goes through all 27 rewards, each with whether it's earned, the next one you're working towards, or locked, for example "Reward 10, New Performer Costume, locked".

## The CUSTOMIXER

The CUSTOMIXER is where you make the act your own. Its "How it works" page sums it up: unlock new costumes to tweak your performers' sounds, adjust their volume to make your own mix, and swap stage decor as you unlock new styles.

### Performers

Each performer has a button and a volume slider.

- The DJ's button says "options" instead of "costumes". In my act that was DJ Epic. The options page has one setting, Motion FX, which turns the screen effects on or off.
- In my round, the other performers' buttons were switched off, I think because they had no costumes to choose from yet. Costumes come from the Hype progress path. Once a performer has some, its button should say "costumes" and open the costume list.
- The sliders say who they belong to and how loud they are, for example "Candelavra volume, slider, 40 percent".
- DJ Epic had two entries in my act: the DJ himself, whose slider says "no sound to mix", and DJ Epic's music track, which you can turn up and down.

### Changing the volume

Tab to a performer's slider, then:

- Shift+Comma turns it down and Shift+Period turns it up, 10 percent at a time. Hold the key to keep going.
- Shift+Home turns it all the way down and Shift+End all the way up.
- Enter puts it back to 100 percent.

The same keys work on the game's other sliders too, such as the Time Warper and decoration size.

### Stage decor

Each piece of stage decor has a button that switches between its styles. The mod says which part of the stage it changes and which style it's on, for example "Stage decor, back stage, style 1 of 2".

- "locked" means you haven't won that piece yet.
- "new style to try" means you've won a new style and haven't looked at it yet.
- Press Enter to switch to the next style. The mod says the new style.

## When the round ends

When the timer runs out, the round is over. Your progress for that act should carry over to the next time the same act comes back, but I haven't seen a round end yet, so I can't say for sure what happens to leftover tickets.
