# Getting started and progressing

What to do once the tutorial is over, and how Keri and I play from day to day. If you haven't finished the tutorial yet, start with tutorial.md.

I wrote this guide from Keri's debug log and my own: the keys we use most, the things we do over and over, and the places we got stuck.

## Finding your way around

- Control+Shift+A says your status: level, island, coins, diamonds and food. Press it whenever you want to know what you can afford.
- Control+Shift+W ("what now") says what is ready (coins, bakery food, eggs, finished breeding) and your current goals, for example "Ready: Monsters have coins to collect. Goals: 1. Breed a Noggin and Potbelly together."
- Control+Shift+O lists everything on your island. Left bracket and Right bracket switch between groups: Buttons, Buildings, Monsters, Decorations, and Trees and rocks. Tab moves through a group, and Enter selects or taps the object.
- Control+Shift+M opens Places: Market, Book of Monsters, Map, Goals, Friends, Mail and Options.
- When something on the island is selected, Tab goes through its actions (for example INFO, MOVE, FEED, COLLECT, MUTE). Escape deselects it: you hear "Deselected".
- On the main screen with nothing selected, Tab goes through the bottom bar (OPTIONS, MARKET, COLLECT ALL once you have it), Goals and the counters at the top.
- Home and End jump to the start and end of a list.
- Control+Shift+C puts the camera back on the whole island if the view gets lost.

## Your daily routine

This is the loop Keri and I both fell into. Each step takes a few key presses.

1. Close the welcome screens. When the game starts it shows news, offers and sometimes "Update Available". Press Escape or Tab to Close. On the Daily Login Bonus, press COLLECT.
2. Press Control+Shift+W to hear what is ready.
3. Collect coins. Before Collect All is unlocked, select each monster from Control+Shift+O and press Enter: selecting a monster collects its coins ("Got 34 coins"). Once Collect All is on the bottom bar, Tab to COLLECT ALL, press Enter and answer Yes.
4. Collect food. Select your Bakery: if food is ready, selecting or tapping it collects it ("Got 25 food").
5. Bake again. With the Bakery selected, Tab to BAKE for the full menu, or to REBAKE to repeat the last bake in every Bakery. Each food item says how much food it makes, how long it takes and what it costs, for example "Cookies, 25 - 5m 0s, 250".
6. Feed your monsters. Select a monster, Tab to FEED, and press Enter. Each press spends food, and the monster's status says "fed 2 of 4". Every 4 feeds it goes up a level, and you hear "(name) leveled up to level 5". Higher-level monsters make more coins.
7. Check Goals. Tab to Goals on the main screen and collect anything that's done. Goals are the fastest way to earn XP, coins and diamonds early on.
8. Breed, hatch and place (see below).

## Breeding

Breeding is how you get new monsters. Many goals ask for a specific pair, for example "Breed a Noggin and Potbelly together".

1. Select the Breeding Structure (Control+Shift+O, Buildings group) and Tab to BREED.
2. There are two lists, "Monster 1 list" and "Monster 2 list". Left bracket and Right bracket jump between them. Monsters that can't breed yet say why, for example "Requires Level 4", and some have a "Feed!" button right in the list.
3. Pick the first monster with Enter. The other list then says which monsters would "breed with" it. Pick the second.
4. You hear "Breeding (first) with (second). Now press Click to Breed, near the top of the list." Press Home, Tab to "Click to Breed" and press Enter.
5. If the game shows a breed warning (for example about the result), choose BREED to go ahead or RESELECT to choose again.
6. Breeding takes time. Control+Shift+W says when it's finished, and the Breeding Structure says "breeding finished, egg ready".
7. Select the Breeding Structure. The screen says "You've bred (monster)". WAIT sends the egg to the Nursery to incubate normally; SPEED UP spends Diamonds to finish it straight away.
8. When the Nursery says "egg ready to hatch", select it, tap it, choose PLACE, then press Enter to place it. SELL is there too if you don't want the monster.

Breeding doesn't always give a new hybrid: sometimes you get a copy of one of the parents. That's normal, so just try again.

## Placing things

Everything you buy or hatch has to be placed on the island.

- When placing starts, the mod tells you where the object is, for example "Free spot, 2 tiles left of Small Tree". If that spot is fine, just press Enter.
- Arrow keys move a placing cursor one tile and say the spot straight away: "Free spot" or "Blocked", what is nearby, and any Likes close by. Nothing moves in the game until you press Enter, so you can arrow around as fast as you like.
- Left bracket and Right bracket jump the cursor to the objects on your island, nearest free spot first. Comma and Period then step through the other free spots next to that object.
- Enter places the object where the cursor is. Escape cancels. Tab says the spot again.
- Monsters next to things they like get happier and make coins faster. The game tells you when that happens.

## Beds and the Castle

Every monster needs beds in your Castle. When the Castle is full, buying or placing says "Your Castle does not have enough Beds. Go to Castle to upgrade now?"

- Select the Castle and Tab to UPGRADE. Upgrading costs coins and takes a few hours, so it can run overnight. The Castle says "upgrading, 3 hours 51 minutes left", and when it is done you hear "The Castle upgrade is finished".
- The Castles go Basic, Modest, Comfortable and up, and each gives more beds.
- The Bakery can be upgraded the same way. A bigger Bakery has more foods to choose from, from Cupcakes up to Bread, Donuts, Ice Cream and Pizza.

## Money tips

- Coins come from monsters. Collect often, level your monsters, and keep them happy.
- Food comes from the Bakery and is spent on feeding. Short bakes like Cupcakes and Cookies are good while you are playing. Long bakes like Bread or Donuts are good before you leave.
- Diamonds are rare. The tutorial and goals give you some, so think before you spend them on speeding things up.
- "You don't have enough Coins" (or Food, Diamonds, Treats) means what it says. Answer No unless you mean to go to the shop.
- Some shop items cost real money, shown as a price like "$9.99 USD". Buying them goes through Steam, which needs the Steam overlay turned on.

## Minigames and extras

These open from buttons on the main screen or from a building's actions.

- Scratch ticket (SCRATCH on the Nursery): Tab to a square and press Enter, or choose "Scratch all squares". Once all six are uncovered, Tab to Collect.
- Memory Game (MEMORY on the Castle): Tab to a card and press Enter to turn it over, and find the matching pairs. The game says the level and the mismatches you have left.
- Colossal Conundrum (CONUNDRUM on the Coloss-Eye): one puzzle piece per day. The screen says how many pieces you have, and Collect appears when a new piece is ready. The Coloss-Eye is a free structure in the Market (STRUCTURES) once the game tells you about it.
- Encore events: the Encore button says your points and the time left, and the event screen explains how to earn points.
- Book of Monsters (in Places): what you have collected on each island, with filters by element.
- Map (in Places): other islands, what they cost and what they need. Picking an island reads its details and puts focus on GO.
- Trees and rocks: goals sometimes ask you to remove one. Select it from the "Trees and rocks" group and use its actions. Removing takes time, and the mod says when it is done.

## When things go wrong

- If speech goes quiet or Tab says "Nothing to navigate in this dialog", press Escape once and try again. Some game screens have nothing to press.
- If a screen speaks something odd or you can't find a button, press Control+Shift+D on that screen. It writes what the mod can see to its log.
- Run debug.bat in the mod folder to get a zip of the logs on your Desktop, and send it to me with a short note about what you were doing. That's how Keri's log helped me make the tutorial playable for everyone.
