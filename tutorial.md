# Tutorial walkthrough

A step-by-step guide to the game's tutorial with My Singing Monsters Access, from "Welcome to the world of My Singing Monsters" to "Tutorial complete".

## Thank you, Keri

Before anything else, I want to thank Keri. She was the first person to play the whole tutorial through with this mod and go on to collect monsters. She kept going through crashes, restarts and steps that wouldn't work, and when she sent me her debug log, it showed me exactly where people got stuck: placing the first monster, renaming the Noggin, building the Bakery. I fixed those because of her, and this guide follows her path through the tutorial. Without her, a lot of people would still be stuck on the first island. Thank you, Keri.

## Before you start

- Install the mod (see the README) and start the game. You hear "My Singing Monsters accessibility mod loaded".
- The tutorial has 78 steps. Most steps either show a message or ask you to do one thing.
- Control+Shift+Enter is your main tutorial key. On a message, it continues. On a step that needs you to do something, it does the obvious first part for you, such as opening the Market or tapping the Nursery, and says what it is doing, for example "Control Shift Enter to tap Nursery, egg ready to hatch".
- Control+Shift+T repeats the current step if you missed it.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move through the items on the current screen, and Enter presses one. Escape or Backspace goes back.
- When a step says "This step needs an action. Tab to find it", press Tab and look for the button it is talking about.
- After most steps, the tutorial asks you to "Collect your reward". The Goals section below explains how; you will do it many times.
- If you get stuck, press Control+Shift+D on the screen you are stuck on, then run debug.bat and send the zip it puts on your Desktop.

## Things you will do again and again

### Collecting a goal reward

1. On the main screen, press Tab until you hear "Goals", then press Enter.
2. The Goals screen says each goal as "Show me: (goal). Reward: (reward)". Tab to Collect and press Enter. You hear what you got, for example "Got 150 coins".
3. Press Escape to close Goals.
4. If you level up, a "Level 2" screen appears with an Okay button. Tab to Okay and press Enter.

### Buying from the Market

1. Press Control+Shift+M for Places, Tab to Market and press Enter. (On "buy" steps, Control+Shift+Enter opens the Market for you.)
2. Tab to MONSTERS or STRUCTURES and press Enter to pick that section.
3. Tab to what you want, for example "Noggin, NEW, 300", and press Enter. You hear its description.
4. Tab to BUY and press Enter. You hear "Spent 300 coins".

### Hatching and placing a monster

1. Press Control+Shift+Enter (or find the Nursery with Control+Shift+O) to tap the Nursery. The screen says "You've hatched (monster). What would you like to do with it?".
2. Tab to PLACE and press Enter. You hear "Placing object. Free spot, ... Arrow keys move it, Enter places it, Escape cancels."
3. The spot it starts on is free, so you don't have to move it. Press Enter. You hear "Placed".
4. To put it somewhere else, use the arrow keys first. Each press moves a cursor one tile and says the spot straight away; the monster only moves when you press Enter.

### Speeding something up with a Diamond

1. Tab to SPEED UP and press Enter.
2. A question says how long is left, for example "Incubation time 1m 50s. Finish now with 1 Diamond(s)?". Tab to Yes and press Enter.
3. Let go of Enter before choosing. The mod holds the answer buttons back while Enter is still down, so a question never answers itself.

## The tutorial, step by step

### 1. Welcome

1. "Welcome to the world of My Singing Monsters!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. "Each Monster you collect adds to your song. Let's start with a Noggin." Press Control+Shift+Enter.

### 2. Buy a Noggin egg

1. The step says "Buy a Noggin egg". Press Control+Shift+Enter to open the Market.
2. Tab to MONSTERS, press Enter, Tab to "Noggin, NEW, 300", press Enter, then Tab to BUY and press Enter.
3. "Monster eggs incubate in the Nursery. The egg will crack when it's hatched and ready to be placed." Press Control+Shift+Enter. The first egg hatches straight away: you hear "An egg finished incubating".

### 3. Place the Monster

1. The step says "Place the Monster". Press Control+Shift+Enter to tap the Nursery.
2. On "You've hatched Noggin", Tab to PLACE and press Enter.
3. Press Enter to place it. You hear "Placed".

### 4. Collect your reward

1. "Congratulations! You completed a goal!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. Collect the "Place your Noggin" goal (see "Collecting a goal reward" above).

### 5. Rename the Noggin

1. "Goals help you level up and unlock new Monsters. Give your Noggin a new name." Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Rename the Noggin". Press Control+Shift+Enter to select your Noggin. It already has a random name, for example "Thwak, Noggin selected. Tab for actions."
3. Tab to INFO and press Enter.
4. Tab to "Rename, now called Thwak" and press Enter.
5. The name box says the current name. Press Backspace until you hear "empty" (each press says the letter it deleted).
6. Type the new name. Each letter is spoken.
7. Press Enter to save. You hear "Saving the name".
8. Press Escape to close the info screen, then collect the "Rename your Noggin" goal. You reach level 2: press Enter on Okay.

### 6. Buy a Mammott egg and speed it up

1. The step says "Now, buy a Mammott egg". Press Control+Shift+Enter to open the Market.
2. MONSTERS, "Mammott, NEW, 300", BUY.
3. "Some Monsters take longer to hatch. Let's use a Diamond to speed it up!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
4. The step says "Speed up". Tab to SPEED UP, press Enter, then answer Yes.
5. Hatch and place the Mammott (see "Hatching and placing a monster" above).
6. Collect the goal reward. You reach level 3: Okay.

### 7. Build a Small Bakery

1. "Wow! A Bakery! We should get one!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Build a Small Bakery". Press Control+Shift+M, open the Market, and this time pick STRUCTURES.
3. Tab to "Small Bakery, NEW, 100", press Enter, then BUY.
4. The Bakery is now being placed: press Enter to place it where it is, or arrow to another free spot first.
5. If the step changes to "Click on the ground to de-select", you selected something by accident. Press Escape to deselect it and the step goes back to the Bakery.

### 8. Bake, speed up and collect Cookies

1. "Now that we have a Bakery, we should make our Monsters some Cookies!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Bake some Cookies". Press Control+Shift+Enter to tap the Small Bakery.
3. Tab to BAKE and press Enter. The bakery lists Cupcakes, Cookies and Bread with the food you get, the time, and the price in coins.
4. Tab to "Cookies, 25 - 5m 0s, 250", press Enter, and answer Yes.
5. The step says "Speed up baking". Tab to SPEED UP, press Enter, answer Yes.
6. The step says "Collect the Treats". Press Control+Shift+Enter to tap the Small Bakery. You hear "Got 25 food".
7. Open Goals and collect both Bakery goals.

### 9. Feed your Noggin up to level 4

1. "Great! Now, feed your Monsters to level them up and unlock new features!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Feed your Noggin up to level 4". Press Control+Shift+Enter to select your Noggin.
3. Tab to FEED and press Enter. You hear "Spent 5 food". Keep pressing Enter on FEED.
4. Every 4 feeds the monster goes up a level, and you hear "(name) leveled up to level 2". The step says how far along you are, for example "level 2, fed 2 of 4".
5. At level 4 the step changes to "Collect your reward". Press Escape to deselect, then collect the goal. You reach level 4: Okay.

### 10. Buy and place a Toe Jammer

1. "We can add even more Monsters to the island!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Buy and place a Toe Jammer". Press Control+Shift+Enter to open the Market, then MONSTERS, "Toe Jammer, NEW, 250", BUY.
3. The step says "Speed up": SPEED UP, Yes.
4. Hatch and place the Toe Jammer.
5. Collect the goal. You reach level 5: Okay.

### 11. Collect Coins from a Monster

1. "Monsters will regularly produce Coins for you." Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Collect Coins from a Monster". Press Control+Shift+O for the island list, press Right bracket until you reach Monsters, Tab to a monster and press Enter.
3. Selecting a monster collects its coins: you hear "Got 34 coins".
4. Press Escape to deselect, then collect the goal. You reach level 6: Okay.

### 12. Feed your Mammott up to level 4

1. "Feeding a second Monster will allow us to breed more Monsters!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. Press Control+Shift+Enter to select the Mammott, then press Enter on FEED until it reaches level 4, the same as the Noggin.
3. Escape, then collect the goal. You reach level 7: Okay.

### 13. Breed your first hybrid

1. "Now that you have 2 Monsters at Level 4, let's learn how to breed Monster hybrids!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The step says "Click on the Breeding Structure". Press Control+Shift+Enter to select it.
3. The step says "Click on the Breed Button". Tab to BREED and press Enter.
4. Four short explanations follow ("The Breeding Menu shows you...", "Monster species", "Monster level", "Monster elements"). Press Control+Shift+Enter for each.
5. The step says "Click on a first Monster". The breeding screen has two lists, "Monster 1 list" and "Monster 2 list"; Left bracket and Right bracket jump between them. Tab to your Noggin and press Enter. You hear "picked as monster 1. Now choose monster 2".
6. Tab to the Mammott (it says "breed with" your Noggin) and press Enter. You hear "Breeding (Noggin) with (Mammott). Now press Click to Breed, near the top of the list."
7. Press Home to jump to the top, Tab to "Click to Breed" and press Enter.
8. "Breeding has begun! Let's use a Diamond to speed it up!" Press Control+Shift+Enter, then Tab to SPEED UP and press Enter.
9. "Congratulations on your first Monster hybrid!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.

### 14. Move the egg to the Nursery

1. The step says "Click on the Egg to transfer it to the Nursery". Press Control+Shift+Enter to tap the Breeding Structure.
2. The screen says "You've bred (monster)". Tab to SPEED UP and press Enter. The egg moves to the Nursery and hatches.
3. Don't use MOVE on the Nursery for this step: MOVE picks the Nursery building up to move it. If you do it by accident, press Escape to cancel the placement.

### 15. Tutorial complete

1. "Tutorial complete! Congratulations!" Press Control+Shift+Enter.
2. The game now shows its usual welcome screens: news, offers, the Daily Login Bonus. Close offers with Escape or Close, and press COLLECT on the Daily Login Bonus.
3. Your hybrid is waiting in the Nursery. Hatch and place it as before. If you place it next to something it likes, you hear that its Happiness went up.

You now have four monsters, a Bakery and level 7. The getting started guide covers what to do next.
